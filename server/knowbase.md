# Helpdesk Knowledge Base

## Account & Login

### Resetting your password
If you can't log in because you've forgotten your password, go to the login page and click
"Forgot password?". Enter the email address associated with your account and we'll send a
password reset link. The link expires after 24 hours. If you don't receive the email within
a few minutes, check your spam folder.

### "Invalid credentials" error
This error means the email or password you entered doesn't match our records. Double check
for typos, extra spaces, or caps lock being on. If you're sure your password is correct, it's
possible your account was created with a different email address than the one you're signing
in with.

## Billing & Refunds

### Refund policy
We offer a full refund within 30 days of purchase, no questions asked. To request a refund,
reply with your order number and we will process it within 3-5 business days back to your
original payment method.

### Cancelling a subscription
You can cancel your subscription at any time from Account Settings > Billing > Cancel
Subscription. Cancelling stops future renewals but does not refund the current billing
period unless it falls within the 30-day refund window above.

### Updating your payment method
Go to Account Settings > Billing > Payment Methods to add or update a card. Changes take
effect on your next billing cycle immediately.

## File Uploads

### Upload size limits
The maximum file upload size is 20MB per file. If an upload fails silently with no error
message on a file under 20MB, try a different browser or clear your browser cache, as this
is sometimes caused by a stale local cache.

### Supported file formats
We support CSV, XLSX, and JSON for data imports. Other formats like PDF or Word documents
are not supported for data import (only as message attachments).

## General

### Response times
Our support team typically responds to tickets within 1 business day. Urgent billing or
account-access issues are prioritized.

### Contacting support
You can always reach us by replying to any ticket email.
